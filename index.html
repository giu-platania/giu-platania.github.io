Dafrion / Digital twin of the organization

See how the business works. Test what to change.

Dafrion models how people, processes, resources, and systems work together. Teams can map operations, assess performance and risk, simulate scenarios before committing resources, and use AI to propose changes while keeping approval with people. Application modernization is one use case alongside strategy execution, operational improvement, and governance.

04 / open source

The other half of the practice

Build in the open. Learn in public.

Open source keeps architecture honest. Constraints are real, interfaces are visible, and usefulness is earned one interaction at a time.

R3AKT brings this work together: REM for field teams, RCH for coordination, and Fabric/LXMF-rs for encrypted communications over LoRa and IP. The result is portable, resilient situational awareness designed for intermittent connectivity rather than permanent cloud access.
